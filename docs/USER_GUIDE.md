# User guide

JobPingly tells you when the companies you want to work for post a job that fits you. It reads their careers pages directly, so you hear about a role when it appears on the company's site.

## Getting started

1. **Create an account** with your email (you will get a 6-digit code) or with Google.
2. **Add companies**: paste the link to each company's careers page. Job-board links work best, for example `jobs.lever.co/company`, `boards.greenhouse.io/company`, `jobs.ashbyhq.com/company` or a Workday careers site; ordinary company careers pages work too. Use **Check** to see what JobPingly finds before adding.
3. **Choose what you want to hear about**: keywords such as job titles or skills, and words to exclude.
4. **Pick how often to get email**. That's it: JobPingly checks the companies every few hours.

Jobs that are already open when you add a company appear in your feed straight away; alerts are only sent for jobs posted after that.

## Watchlists

A watchlist is a group of companies with its own alert settings, for example "Remote fintech" or "Dream companies".

- **Companies tab**: add, import (paste many links, or a CSV exported from another watchlist), export, pause a company on this list, change its link or category, or remove it. Each company shows whether monitoring is working, its open jobs and when it was last checked.
- **Your alerts tab**: your keywords and filters for this list (see below), with a live preview of which open jobs match right now and why.
- **Public watchlists** appear in the Discover directory once they contain a company. Other people can follow them and set their own keywords; your keywords are never shown to anyone. People can also suggest companies, which you approve or reject.
- **Members**: invite people by email as editors (add and remove companies) or moderators (also edit details and review suggestions).
- **Settings**: name, description, tags, public or private, pin to the top, delete.

## Alert rules

- **Any of these**: a job matches if it contains at least one of the keywords. Add an **"and" group** to also require one keyword from another group, for example *frontend, react* and *senior, lead*.
- **Never if the title contains**: excludes jobs whose title has any of these words.
- **Look for keywords in**: job title, department, location and optionally the description.
- **Match whole words only** (recommended): "java" will not match "javascript".
- **Filters**: workplace (remote, hybrid, on-site), employment type, level and locations. Many postings do not state these; choose whether such jobs should still count.
- Leave everything empty to hear about every new job at the list's companies.

## Email and notifications

- **Settings → Notifications**: turn alert emails on or off, choose how often (as soon as a job is found, every 6 or 12 hours, daily, twice a week, weekly, every two weeks, monthly or a custom interval), the time digests are sent and your time zone. Each watchlist can use your default or its own frequency.
- An email is only sent when there is something new. Every alert email has an unsubscribe link; you can turn emails back on at any time.
- New accounts' alert emails may need a quick approval first; until then, matches still appear in the app.
- **In-app notifications** (the bell) cover new matches, changes to jobs you saved, and problems with your companies; you can switch each type off.

## Jobs

The Jobs page has views for matching jobs, everything new this week, all open jobs, recently closed jobs and jobs you dismissed. Search and filter by watchlist, company, workplace, employment type, level and posting date. Save a job to your tracker, hide one you are not interested in, or open the posting on the company's site. A job's page shows its details, why it matched, its history (for example when the salary or location changed) and your tracking notes.

## Tracker

Follow your applications from Saved to Applied, Interviewing, Offer and Closed, on a board or as a list. Add notes, the date you applied and a reminder; JobPingly reminds you in the app (and by email if you allow it). You can also add jobs you found elsewhere, and export everything to CSV.

## When a company shows a problem

- **Waiting for first scan**: it was just added; results appear within a few minutes.
- **Having trouble** or **Failing**: the hint explains why (for example the site was too slow, or the page needs a browser to show its jobs). JobPingly keeps retrying automatically. If the company moved its jobs to another site, change the link from the company's menu in your watchlist; using the company's job-board link usually helps.
- **Check now** asks for a fresh check (at most once every 10 minutes per company).

## Your account

**Settings** lets you change your name, photo, time zone and public links, your password (or set one if you use Google), see where you are signed in and sign out other devices, download all your data as JSON, and delete your account permanently.

## Plans

JobPingly is free. The free plan has limits on the number of watchlists, companies per watchlist and keywords per alert (shown in Settings and on the Pricing page). Higher limits are available on request from the Help page.
